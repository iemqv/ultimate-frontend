# ОТТИСК
## Brief
Russian-language independent print workshop, for print collectors, curious beginners and artists who need a small edition. Job: discover techniques, see example editions, and learn how to contact the workshop. Name, editions and workshop offers are demo content; no address, calendar or real credentials supplied. Static HTML/CSS/JS: the private workspace requires a direct index.html and has no shell/package manager. Canvas used for original local print illustrations, not interaction infrastructure.

## Concepts
1. Material: an ink proof lying on a press bed. Registration marks and paper margins align the sections; tap to lift the ink and compare the relief and the print. Feeling: tactile curiosity. Frame: an orange ammonite in a grey tray. Anchor: a relief proof is pulled from a carved block. Originality / fit / feasibility / portability: 4/5/5/2.
2. Name: «Оттиск» leaves a repeating impression. Edition numbers and repeated motifs compose the index, with a stamp landing on each selection. Feeling: decisiveness. Frame: big overlapping stamps. Anchor: оттиск means an individual impression. 3/5/5/3.
3. Process: a wall of drying prints. Staggered sheets settle vertically; opening an edition takes it off the wall. Feeling: workshop quiet. Frame: an offset pair of sheets with notes. Anchor: individual inked sheets need drying. 3/4/4/2.

## Concept card
Idea: Pull an ink proof from the press bed.
Metaphor: relief printing proof sheet, then an edition index.
Signature: the proof toggles to a mirrored, monochromatic printing block, settling into the same registration marks.
Emotion: hands-on curiosity.
Motion: lift, shift, re-register; quiet and still at rest like a print on a table. Reduced motion turns transitions off.
Screenshot: saturated flame-orange ammonite cut opposite oversized black Cyrillic.

## System
Stock #f7f6f2, plate #e9e6de, ink #181817, secondary #57544e, line #d3cfc5, cobalt #233edc. Print colours independent: orange, indigo, forest. Arial local grotesk for compact Cyrillic headings and UI, system mono for production notes. 12-column alignment described by wide split and asymmetric pairs, 4 on phones. Square corners throughout, button fill and short registration-shift feedback, cubic ease-out. Hero 2 columns; works an asymmetric edition wall with filters; workshop an index of techniques; contact a cobalt statement footer. Genericness test: replaced stock photo and gradient overlays with custom procedural relief printing, registration tray and an interactive mirrored block.
