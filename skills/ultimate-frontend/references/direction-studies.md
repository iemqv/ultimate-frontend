# Direction studies: borrow a decision, not a look

Use these as studies in taste, not live descriptions of a site's current homepage. The upstream Taste Skill points to [Figma](https://www.figma.com/), [Figure](https://www.figure.ai/) and [1X](https://www.1x.tech/) as references. A reference earns its place when you can explain which relationship is worth borrowing and which surface detail must stay behind. You don't need to browse or imitate a brand to use this method.

| Study | Decision to borrow | Translation to a different brief | Wrong takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Figma as a working canvas | Let a visitor manipulate the evidence instead of reading a claim about it. | A route-planning tool can let a visitor move one stop and immediately see what changed. The result is the composition. | Fake app windows, invented collaborators, cursors as decoration. |
| Figure or 1X as an object study | The relationship between a whole object and a well-chosen detail can explain more than a grid of selling points. | An instrument maker can start with the silhouette, then reveal one joint at a scale that makes its construction legible. | Render everything in dark chrome, make every page full-bleed. |
| A performance score | An alignment rule can let the eye compare events over time without a paragraph of explanation. | An incident timeline can put the affected service and response directly against the same time edge. | Staves, conductor metaphors, illegible microtype. |
| A field guide | Variation is visible because examples share a viewpoint; the difference is the content. | For a material library, show comparable samples at a consistent scale. Let one chosen sample expand to reveal the reason to select it. | Specimen numbers, stamps and archival paper on every project. |

**Select by the job.** For an experience, put the work on stage and let visitors approach it; for persuasion, place proof beside the doubt it resolves; for operations, put the live choice and its effect in the same view; for reading, compose a sustained reading measure and meaningful entry points. A highly conventional invoice screen with a clear exception state is a success. A portfolio whose strongest visual is its navigation is not.

**Choose what varies.** Note the actual material and its provenance, the dominant shape or working edge, the narrow-screen treatment, and what changes from opening to next state. Look for a property of the material that changes the composition: scale, sequence, comparison, proximity, or another relationship the visitor can perceive. Don't give every site a reveal; sometimes a single well-cropped image is enough. Imagine the shapes at postage-stamp size, then read the words at normal size.

If two different structures could serve the task, compare them with the same material in a few lines of notes, a sketch, or a thumbnail. Render a second study only if the decision depends on something you can't judge in the sketch. Name why the selected relationship is better for this visitor, and build it in the first working render. Don't audition against an approved comp.
