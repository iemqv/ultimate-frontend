# Build the frame around the evidence

## Make the expensive part first

Identify the region that carries the design. Give it actual content at delivery quality before constructing a page around it. A tiny preview nested inside a shadowed panel inside another container is rarely evidence at a useful scale.

- **Physical place or person:** use supplied or appropriately licensed photography when it is available. Inspect the depicted subject, rights, resolution, crop and focal point. Do not pass stock off as this client. Without suitable photography, choose a direction that can be true without it: a map of supplied places, a readable program, a typographic work, an original illustration. Never leave a photo slot waiting on a network URL.
- **Creative work:** author or curate actual works a visitor could distinguish from one another. Give each a specific formal idea, not the same procedural asset in three colors. Decide what should be seen whole, what benefits from a detail, and which edges may be cut. When making vector art, compose intentional shapes, depth, silhouette and negative space. Noise laid over basic geometry is not a substitute for drafting the subject. Show the work bigger than the furniture that labels it.
- **Product or service:** show the thing happening. Implement a small but real operation with plausible input and a visible result, or put the decision-changing fact where the choice is made. No fake terminal animation, dashboard facsimile or clickable controls painted into an image. For a service with missing business facts, make the method or relevant choices clear instead of inventing guarantees.
- **Information or tool:** start from a representative hard case: long labels, sparse and dense data, an exception. Build the working row, comparison or reading measure before the shell. Use repetition when it enables comparison; interrupt it only for a meaningful exception.

## Set the frame

Choose the primary edge or axis. Place content by its relationship to that edge; give the focal region a deliberate extent and the labels a quieter register readable at normal zoom. Specify one crossing of scale or density between the first and next beat. Build with semantic source order and fluid dimensions. The phone frame is a new crop and order, not a vertical stack of every desktop introduction followed by the substance. Keep the work and its most important affordance in reach.

Check an actual long title and a short one in the available font **in the requested script**. Compare adjacent roles, not font names: a display face may carry the voice, but body and controls must remain comfortable. Set the type hierarchy, text measure, gutters, quiet surfaces and emphasis as shared CSS roles. Size the frame to content; don't use an arbitrary viewport height that clips an item at laptop height. If photography or an expressive typeface is unavailable, solve with material and composition, not five new decorative effects.

## Hold the first frame against the lock

At desktop and phone widths, inspect what lands before scrolling: which shape dominates, which evidence is legible, which action looks possible. Then inspect the next beat. If your declared subject is merely an inset to a large introduction, the artifact was demoted. Promote it in the layout or make the introductory words part of the work, rather than shrinking them a little. If a second section looks like a stock block from a different site, use the first frame's organizing relationship to answer its new question. Don't repeat the entire hero at a smaller size.

Only then spend time on motion and polish. Preserve the stable first frame with JS disabled where practical and reduced motion enabled. Content that enters only after a timer, hover, scroll animation or essential network font has not arrived yet.
