# Holdout / Open — Envelope Garden

Evaluator-only. Give each arm the same `prompt.txt` bytes and a fresh copy of `site/`; do not give it this test. Evaluate the resulting site, not this starter. Serve the result over local HTTP, allowing the local origin only for required resources. Use 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 CSS-pixel viewports. Record pass/fail and an observation for each check.

## Browser checks

1. In a fresh page load, find Envelope Garden, the April 1–10 drop-off dates, Reed Street Community Hall's front desk, and free participation. The page says up to two envelopes, open-pollinated flowers or vegetables only, no treated or wild-collected seeds, and both plant name and harvest year on each envelope. One envelope may be taken for each left, while supplies last.
2. At both widths all required information is readable without horizontal page scrolling and without relying on an external request. No interaction is required; if controls are presented, check that they are reachable and work.

## Human check

Read as a first-time participant. Can you prepare a compliant envelope, find the drop-off, and tell what comes back with you? Record one concrete instruction that was easy or hard to find. Accept any wording or presentation that preserves those facts. Do not score by component choice, design style, or exact copy.
