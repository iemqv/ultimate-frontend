Build a one-page site for Envelope Garden, a spring seed swap at Reed Street Community Hall. The page should help a new neighbor prepare an eligible envelope and know what will happen when they drop it off.

Drop-offs are April 1–10 at the hall’s front desk. Bring up to two envelopes of open-pollinated flower or vegetable seeds. Label each envelope with the plant name and harvest year. Please don’t bring treated or wild-collected seed. For every envelope you leave, you may take one from the swap box while supplies last. Taking part is free.

Work in the supplied site folder. Make the page usable on a phone and a desktop without fetching anything from the internet.
