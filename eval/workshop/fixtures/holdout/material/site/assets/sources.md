# Snow in Three Prints — image sources

All three works are color woodblock prints by Utagawa Hiroshige (Japanese, 1797–1858). The files are unmodified web JPEGs from the Cleveland Museum of Art's [Open Access program](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access). The museum marks the reproductions [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); they may be copied and redistributed. Credit the museum for the reproductions.

## kambara.jpg

**Evening Snow at Kambara, 1833.** From *Fifty-three Stations of the Tokaido*. Small white flakes stand out against the dark sky as travelers pass snow-covered roofs. Hiroshige used unprinted areas of the paper to show the flakes.

- The Cleveland Museum of Art, 1985.317, The Kelvin Smith Collection, given by Mrs. Kelvin Smith: [object record](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1985.317) · [JPEG](https://openaccess-cdn.clevelandart.org/1985.317/1985.317_web.jpg)

## asuka-hill.jpg

**Evening Snow at Asuka Hill, c. 1837–38.** From *Eight Views of the Environs of Edo*. Falling snow dots both the dusk sky and a hillside of snow-laden trees; small figures and a pack animal travel below.

- The Cleveland Museum of Art, 1940.983, Bequest of James Parmelee: [object record](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1940.983) · [JPEG](https://openaccess-cdn.clevelandart.org/1940.983/1940.983_web.jpg)

## matsuchiyama.jpg

**Clear Weather after Snow at Matsuchiyama, late 1830s or early 1840s.** From *Famous Places in the Eastern Capital*. Here the snow has settled: roofs and a low bank remain white above blue water, with boats and a bridge in view.

- The Cleveland Museum of Art, 1921.1276, Gift from J. H. Wade: [object record](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1921.1276) · [JPEG](https://openaccess-cdn.clevelandart.org/1921.1276/1921.1276_web.jpg)
