# Three Looks at Neptune — image sources

Voyager 2 flew past Neptune in August 1989. The photographs below are NASA/JPL mission imagery, credited to NASA/JPL in their Photojournal records. The bundled files are unmodified JPEGs from the NASA Image and Video Library. These mission images are generally not subject to U.S. copyright and may be reused here for educational or informational purposes under [NASA's images and media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). ## neptune-storms.jpg

**Neptune’s Great Dark Spot.** This view was reconstructed from two Voyager 2 images. The Great Dark Spot is the large oval near the top; a bright cloud feature nicknamed Scooter and a second dark spot are farther south.

- NASA/JPL, PIA00049: [Photojournal record](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/neptune-great-dark-spot-scooter-dark-spot-2/) · [JPEG](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/PIA00049/PIA00049~orig.jpg)

## neptune-rings.jpg

**Arcs in Neptune’s outermost ring.** The ring material is not spread evenly: in this Voyager 2 image from the August 1989 encounter, some of it is concentrated in three arcs.

- NASA/JPL, PIA01493: [Photojournal record](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/neptunes-rings-3/) · [JPEG](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/PIA01493/PIA01493~orig.jpg)

## neptune-triton.jpg

**Crescents of Neptune and Triton.** Voyager 2 recorded the sunlit crescents of the planet and its moon about three days after its closest approach to Neptune, as the spacecraft was departing.

- NASA/JPL, PIA02215: [Photojournal record](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/crescents-of-neptune-and-triton/) · [JPEG](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/PIA02215/PIA02215~orig.jpg)
