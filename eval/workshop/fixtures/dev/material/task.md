# Dev / Material — Three Looks at Neptune

Evaluator-only. Give each arm the same `prompt.txt` bytes and a fresh copy of `site/`; do not give it this test. Evaluate the resulting site, not this starter. Serve the result over local HTTP, allowing the local origin only for required resources. Use 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 CSS-pixel viewports. Record pass/fail and an observation for each check.

## Browser checks

1. Locate the three supplied images by appearance and verify they visibly render from local files: the view with the Great Dark Spot (PIA00049), the ring arcs (PIA01493), and the two crescents (PIA02215). Local derivatives are acceptable if they preserve the relevant image content. Do not assume a DOM selector, image order, or viewing mechanism.
2. The exhibit connects all three images to Voyager 2's August 1989 encounter. Captions identify the Great Dark Spot as a Neptune feature, the arcs as material concentrated in a ring, and the crescents as Neptune and its moon Triton viewed after closest approach. NASA/JPL credit clearly applies to all three photographs.
3. At both widths all three exhibits and captions can be reached without horizontal page scrolling or internet access. Each image has an accessible text equivalent; if a viewer is needed to reach any exhibit, check it can be used with the keyboard.

## Human check

Read as a visitor new to the mission. Can you match each caption to its own image and locate the feature described? Record a specific detail the site helps you notice or makes hard to see. Judge legibility and interpretation, not composition, layout choice, or exact wording.
