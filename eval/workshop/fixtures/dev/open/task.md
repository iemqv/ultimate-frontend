# Dev / Open — Folding Table Chess

Evaluator-only. Give each arm the same `prompt.txt` bytes and a fresh copy of `site/`; do not give it this test. Evaluate the resulting site, not this starter. Serve the result over local HTTP, allowing the local origin only for required resources. Use 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 CSS-pixel viewports. Record pass/fail and an observation for each check.

## Browser checks

1. In a fresh page load, find the meetup name and purpose, free drop-in participation, the second Sunday of each month, 2–4 p.m., and Narrows Library's East Room. The page makes clear that beginners (including returning players) are welcome, boards and pieces are provided, and visitors can show up and ask for a game without signing up.
2. At both widths all required information is readable without horizontal page scrolling and without relying on an external request. No interaction is required; if controls are presented, check that they are reachable and work.

## Human check

Read as someone deciding whether to go. Can you tell where and when to arrive and what to do on arrival without guessing? Record one concrete fact that was easy or hard to find. Accept any wording or presentation that preserves those facts. Do not score by component choice, design style, or exact copy.
