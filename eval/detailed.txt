Build the public observation planner for Low Lantern, a fictional dark-sky reserve. A visitor has 90 minutes and wants to choose a viewing site. Show four sites with walking time, terrain and a suitable sky target. Let them filter for step-free access, compare two sites, and select one for a visit summary they can clear. Include what to bring. Use illustrative conditions, not live forecasts. All actions must work offline, on a phone and with a keyboard.
